University MV Shooting Project

×

Participate mainly as a producer (including schedule preparation, script and storyboard conception and props), supporting role performances, etc. The story is about a boy dreaming to inherit his father's job as a hand-painted ceramics master, but he gave up to be a sales due to father's sudden death and he desperately needed to support the family's finances. Utilizing Gobo and broken ceramics to represent the protagonist's disillusioned dream. Some framing angles were referenced from Thai singer Billkin's 'Skyline' MV and Japanese singer Suda Masaki(菅田将暉)'s 'Machigaisagashi(まちがいさがし)' MV

Project: Endy Chow Kwok-yin's song "Death of a Salesman" (推銷員之死)
